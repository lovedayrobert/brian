# Brian: what it is and what I'm asking for

## What it is
Brian is a marketing tool I've built for Cashy, powered by Claude. It does two jobs. It finds the moments the internet is talking about while they're still rising, and it turns the best ones into finished campaigns the team can start on the same day. The aim is ideas that get talked about without us paying for the attention, the way Stake, Duel, Housebets and Shuffle got talked about, and a production line that gets them out while the moment still matters.

## How it works

**1. It watches (always on).** Several times a day Brian sweeps news, Google's trending searches, crypto and iGaming press, gaming and culture sites and prediction markets. It groups what it finds into trends. For each trend it measures whether it's emerging, rising, peaking or fading, and shows the evidence: the attention curve, the outlets covering it and the links. Anything involving deaths, disasters, violence or minors is blocked before it can become an idea.

**2. It invents.** For a trend, Brian drafts ten ideas, each built on a single twist that only works for a crypto casino. A deliberately harsh critic then kills most of them. It tests each one: can you explain it in one sentence, would people screenshot it, would press write about it unprompted, would people argue about it, and could Stake or Shuffle run it just as well? Survivors are checked against our legal and safety red lines, and against the web to see whether anyone has done it before. What's left are a few idea cards, each with the headline press would write and the likely backlash spelled out.

**3. It reacts fast.** When something breaks, one tap gives three reactive takes within a minute. Approving one produces ready-to-post copy for X, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram and Discord within the hour. Nothing is ever posted automatically.

**4. It develops (only when we choose to).** When we pick an idea, Brian builds the full package an agency would hand over:
- an executive summary
- the insight and competitor landscape
- the strategy
- creative and copy for every channel
- mockups of every asset inside real X, Instagram, TikTok and landing-page frames
- a channel plan
- a business case
- a production plan with owners, dates and approval gates
- a measurement plan
- risk and compliance
- a Linear project with every task fully briefed

Typing a change like "cut the budget in half" or "drop TikTok" updates the whole package so it stays consistent.

## Why it can be trusted
Every claim links to its source. Anything without a source is flagged, not hidden. Every number is labelled as our own data, a sourced benchmark or an assumption, and the forecast is calculated by code, not guessed by AI. Brian also reports on itself: I tested its trend forecasting against four months of history, and right now it's no better than simple momentum. It says so on screen. That improves as it gets better data and real campaign results.

## What it costs
It runs at two speeds, so there are two costs (estimates, to be confirmed with each provider):
- **Always-on radar: about $400–750 a month** on the Lean setup (free sources plus X's basic API, the AI and hosting).
- **Campaign builds: about $5–30 each**, paid only when we develop one. A month with no new campaign costs nothing here.

Broader coverage costs more. Standard adds Reddit, deeper Google Trends, on-chain data and competitor crawling. Full adds X's Pro API (about $5,000 a month) and TikTok and Instagram via a listening vendor. I'd start on Lean and only move up if it earns it.
